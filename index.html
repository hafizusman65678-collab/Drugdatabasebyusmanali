About PharmaDrug

PharmaDrug is an offline-first pharmacology drug information database designed for pharmacy and medical students.

All data is stored and processed entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no internet required after the first load.

⬇ Download Full Website (ZIP)

Educational use only. This website is intended for pharmacy/medical education and drug-information study. It is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional, official prescribing information, or a pharmacist/physician's advice. Drug doses and clinical recommendations may vary by patient, indication, formulation, country, and current guidelines. Always verify clinical information using authoritative references before making treatment decisions.

Built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Data structured for easy expansion.
